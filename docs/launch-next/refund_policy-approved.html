General returns

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase from AllThings3DShop. If for any reason you are not happy with your order, we offer a 14-day return policy to ensure your satisfaction.

Conditions for Returns:

  1. Eligibility: Items must be returned within 14 days of the delivery date.
  2. Original Condition: Products must be unused, undamaged, and in their original packaging.
  3. Proof of Purchase: A receipt or proof of purchase is required for all returns.
  4. Non-Returnable Items: Certain items, such as custom-made products or digital downloads, are non-returnable. These will be clearly marked on the product page.

How to Initiate a Return:

  1. Contact Us: Email our customer support team at support@teklostudio.com with your order number and reason for the return.
  2. Approval: Once your return is approved, we will provide you with the return address and instructions.
  3. Shipping: You are responsible for the return shipping costs. We recommend using a trackable shipping service or purchasing shipping insurance.
  4. Refund Process: Once we receive your returned item and inspect it, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund.
  5. Refund Timeline: If approved, your refund will be processed, and a credit will automatically be applied to your original method of payment within 7-10 business days.

Additional Information:

  • Exchanges: If you need to exchange an item, please contact us. Exchanges are subject to product availability.
  • Damaged or Defective Items: If your item arrives damaged or defective, please contact us immediately with photos of the damage. We will arrange for a replacement or refund at no additional cost to you.

If you have any questions about our return policy, please don't hesitate to reach out to our customer support team. Your satisfaction is our top priority!
